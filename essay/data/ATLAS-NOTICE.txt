The atlas subset in virtual-worm-atlas.json and the geometry used in
connectome-touch.mp4 derive from the Virtual Worm model built by Chris Grove
for WormBase at the California Institute of Technology.
https://caltech.wormbase.org/virtualworm/

Cuticle and organs were extracted from the Virtual Worm March 2011 mesh
distributed with Blender2NeuroML at commit 598081825349ed72de788f005db5b30ff5168ffa.
https://github.com/openworm/Blender2NeuroML/tree/598081825349ed72de788f005db5b30ff5168ffa

Neuron morphologies come from c302 at commit d542a44bf2662f79e0ad23342a959a181a920877.
https://github.com/openworm/c302/tree/d542a44bf2662f79e0ad23342a959a181a920877
